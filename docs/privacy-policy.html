SehatSaathi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This page is for Google Play Store requirements. The full policy is also available inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy.

1. Overview

SehatSaathi is an offline family health companion. We do not require an account and do not upload your health data to our servers by default.

2. Data on your device

Information you enter (medicines, water, vitals, mood, family profiles, emergency contacts, appointments) is stored locally on your phone.

3. What we do not collect

We do not sell your data, use advertising SDKs, track your location, or read your contacts/SMS. No cloud backup unless you export a JSON file yourself.

4. Permissions

Notifications and exact alarms are used only for reminders you configure. Media access is used only when you pick a backup file to restore.

5. Sharing

When you share a backup, emergency card, or report, you choose the app (WhatsApp, email, etc.). We do not receive those files.

6. Medical disclaimer

This app does not measure vitals and does not provide medical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional.

7. Children

Not directed at children under 13 to use alone. Parents may manage family profiles on their device.

8. Deletion

Delete data in the app or uninstall SehatSaathi to remove all local data from your device.
